filled_region | {sketch: [[[x,y],[x,y]],…], view?, type?, line_style?: name | null} a filled region in a view (a plan, a section, an elevation or a sheet, in its own terms; the lowest plan by default): the loops its sketch closes into (see `sketch`: any number of outlines, a loop inside one a hole, one inside that an island), filled as type `type` (Solid Black unless told) says, its boundary in line style `line_style` (Thin Lines unless told; null, unseen: a masking region's way). It draws over the model, under the annotation; edit its sketch with edit_boundary. A model pattern starts at the region's `pattern_origin` and turns by its `pattern_angle` (see modify)
create_group | {ids, name?} a detail group made of detail lines, filled regions and text notes of one view, as Revit's Create Group: they become type `name`'s members (Group 1, 2… unless told), and one group of it takes their place, its origin the middle of them. Place it again with detail_group; ungroup gives its members back. Returns {id, name}
